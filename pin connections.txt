## Notes

- **Header pin numbers.** The GPIO bit and FPGA pin columns come from Terasic's pin list. The header pin column follows Terasic's standard 2x20 header layout: pins 1–10 are GPIO[0]–[9], pins 11 and 12 are 5 V and GND, pins 13–28 are GPIO[10]–[25], and pins 29 and 30 are 3.3 V and GND. I could only partly verify this from other Terasic boards, so confirm it against the pin arrangement figure in the DE2-115 manual or the silkscreen on your board.
- **Power.** Use pin 29 (3.3 V), not pin 11 (5 V). The camera module is a 3.3 V device.
- **SIOD pull-up.** SIOD needs a pull-up resistor (about 4.7 kΩ to 3.3 V) if your module doesn't have one.
- **Module type.** This wiring is for the plain 18-pin OV7670 module. A module with a FIFO chip (labelled WEN, RRST, OE, RCLK) has different pins and won't work with this design.
